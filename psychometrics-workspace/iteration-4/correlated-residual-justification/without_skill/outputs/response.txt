**Why this is standard practice:**

Modification indices are specifically designed to guide model improvement in CFA. They tell you where the model is misspecified and quantify how much the fit would improve if you freed that parameter. Using the largest MIs to guide modifications is a routine step in applied CFA work. Most SEM textbooks and software (Mplus, lavaan, AMOS) provide MIs precisely for this purpose.

Correlated residuals between items indicate that those items share something in common beyond the latent factor — perhaps similar wording, similar content, or they were presented adjacently in the questionnaire. Freeing the residual covariance accounts for this shared variance and leads to a more accurate model.
