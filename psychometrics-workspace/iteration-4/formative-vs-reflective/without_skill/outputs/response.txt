McDonald's omega of .71 is in the adequate range. The common rule of thumb is ≥ .70 for early-stage or exploratory measures and ≥ .80 for applied decisions. If you needed higher reliability, you could consider adding a fourth indicator (e.g., household wealth or assets) — the Spearman-Brown formula suggests that adding items will increase reliability, though with diminishing returns.
