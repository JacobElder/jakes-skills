KEY PRINCIPLES BEHIND THIS ADVICE

1. LOW COSTS ABOVE ALL ELSE
   Every dollar paid in fees is a dollar not compounding for 35 years. At a 7% average return, $1 today becomes ~$10.68 in 35 years. Fidelity's ZERO funds cost nothing, which is unmatched.

2. BROAD DIVERSIFICATION
   Owning thousands of stocks eliminates the risk of any single company or sector collapsing your portfolio. You own "the whole haystack."

3. TOTAL MARKET INDEX FUNDS BEAT MOST ACTIVE MANAGERS
   Over 15-20+ year periods, roughly 90%+ of actively managed funds underperform their benchmark index after fees. You win by not trying to beat the market.

4. STAY THE COURSE
   The biggest risk is panic-selling during downturns. In a 35-year period you will experience multiple 30–50% market crashes. Do nothing. Keep contributing. This is how wealth is built.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Step 1: Log into Fidelity and select your IRA account.

Step 2: Invest the $6,500. If going with the two-fund option:
   - Buy $5,200 of FZROX
   - Buy $1,300 of FZILX

Step 3: Set up automatic annual contributions. The 2024/2025 IRA limit is $7,000/year (for under age 50). Max it out every year if possible. Automate this.

Step 4: Set up automatic dividend reinvestment (usually on by default at Fidelity).

Step 5: Rebalance once per year if your allocation drifts more than 5–10% from your target. At your age and with automatic contributions, this is optional — your new contributions will naturally rebalance.

Step 6: Do nothing else. Ignore market news. Do not check more than once per quarter.

---

WHAT TO AVOID

- Actively managed funds (higher fees, worse long-term performance)
- Sector funds or thematic ETFs (too concentrated)
- Annuities inside an IRA (unnecessary fees; IRAs already have tax advantages)
- Market timing or trying to "buy the dip"
- Checking your balance too frequently (leads to emotional decisions)
