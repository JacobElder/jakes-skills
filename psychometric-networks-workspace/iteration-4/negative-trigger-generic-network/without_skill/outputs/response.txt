## Betweenness Centrality

**Betweenness centrality** measures how often a node sits on the *shortest path* between other pairs of nodes in a network. It answers the question: **"How much does this node act as a bridge or bottleneck?"**

### The Formula (intuitively)

For each node *v*, look at every pair of other nodes (*s*, *t*) and ask: what fraction of the shortest paths between *s* and *t* pass through *v*? Sum that up across all pairs.

- A node with **high betweenness** sits on many shortest paths → it's a critical connector.
- A node with **low betweenness** is on the periphery → removing it wouldn't disrupt much.

---

### Concrete Example: Airline Hub Networks

Imagine a flight network where nodes are cities and edges are direct routes.

```
Seattle ── Chicago ── New York
              │
           Dallas ── Miami
              │
           Houston
```

**Chicago** has high betweenness centrality — nearly every cross-country trip routes through it. If Chicago's airport shuts down, travel between the coasts and the South becomes severely disrupted.

This is exactly why airlines build **hub-and-spoke** models around high-betweenness cities.

---

### Other Real-World Use Cases

| Domain | What High Betweenness Signals |
|---|---|
| **Social networks** | Influential "brokers" who connect otherwise separate communities |
| **Internet infrastructure** | Routers whose failure would partition the network |
| **Supply chains** | Suppliers or warehouses that are single points of failure |
| **Neuroscience** | Brain regions that integrate signals across modules |
| **Epidemiology** | "Super-spreader" individuals who bridge social clusters |

---

### Key Nuance

Betweenness centrality is distinct from **degree centrality** (just counting connections). A node can have *few* direct connections but *enormous* betweenness if it's the only bridge between two clusters — think of a single bridge connecting two islands. That bridge has degree 2 but controls all traffic between the islands.

This makes betweenness especially valuable for identifying **vulnerability** and **control** in a network, not just popularity.
