Your co-author is invoking a very common but **incorrect** heuristic. Let me break down why, and then give you the right test.

---

## Why overlapping CIs ≠ "not significantly different"

This is one of the most persistent misconceptions in applied statistics. Individual CIs can **substantially overlap** and the difference can still be statistically significant — and vice versa.

The intuition: your individual CI for node A answers *"where does A's true centrality plausibly lie?"* It says nothing directly about the difference A − B. The CI for the **difference** is what you need, and it has its own (often much narrower) uncertainty.

Roughly, for two independent normal estimates, their individual 95% CIs can overlap by up to ~29% of their half-widths and still yield a significant difference at p < .05. The individual CI overlap heuristic corresponds to something closer to α ≈ .005, not .05 — so it's wildly conservative.

---

## Why it's *especially* wrong in network analysis

There's a second, network-specific problem your co-author is missing: **node centralities are not independent estimates**. Strength for node A and Strength for node B are both computed from the *same* network estimated from the *same* data. In any given bootstrap resample, fluctuations in the data tend to push both centralities in correlated directions.

The variance of the quantity you actually care about is:

$$\text{Var}(\text{Strength}_A - \text{Strength}_B) = \text{Var}(A) + \text{Var}(B) - 2\,\text{Cov}(A, B)$$

If A and B are positively correlated across bootstrap resamples (common when they share edges or respond similarly to data perturbations), the CI on the *difference* is **narrower** than what you'd infer by eyeballing the individual CIs. Your co-author is ignoring that covariance term entirely.

---

## The correct test: bootstrap distribution of the difference

Assuming you're using `bootnet` in R (the standard for this workflow), the right procedure is:

```r
# Step 1: Run nonparametric bootstrap (you've likely already done this)
boot_np <- bootnet(network, nBoots = 1000, type = "nonparametric", 
                   statistics = "strength")

# Step 2: Formal difference test — compares bootstrap distributions jointly
differenceTest(boot_np, "A", "B", statistic = "strength")

# Step 3: Visual matrix — shows which pairs significantly differ
plot(boot_np, statistics = "strength", plot = "difference")
```

`differenceTest()` works by computing Strength_A − Strength_B *within each bootstrap resample*, building the empirical sampling distribution of that difference, and checking whether zero falls outside the central 95% of that distribution. This correctly handles the covariance between the two estimates.

The difference plot gives you a lower-triangular matrix: a grey square means the row node is not significantly more central than the column node; a white square means it is. This is the visualization to cite, not the individual CI plot.
